## 4. Brand Typography & Geometry Notes

- **Wordmark Typography**: `Inter`, weight `800` (ExtraBold), letter-spacing `-20` (tight optical kerning).
- **Wordmark Neutral Color**: `#22242B` (Graphite Black).
- **Canonical Usage Rules**:
  - The horizontal logo (`croviq-logo-horizontal.svg`) is the standard identity for all application top-bars and web navigation.
  - The standalone emblem (`croviq-symbol.svg`) is used for square avatars, collapsed sidebars, and favicons.
  - Never stretch, rotate, recolor individual facets, or place the multi-color emblem on conflicting saturated backgrounds.
